## Escalation Process

1. Create a blocker entry as soon as progress is materially impeded.
2. If unresolved within one business day, escalate to the project owner or technical lead.
3. Reflect blocker impact in `STATUS.md` and related task state in `PLAN.md`.
4. When resolved, mark the blocker as `Resolved` and add the resolution steps to `BITACORA.md`.
